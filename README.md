# Sovtiva

**Sovtiva** (styled **sovtiva**) is an independent digital solutions and creative studio based in Mexico City, serving businesses remotely in Mexico and other markets.

**Official website:** https://sovtiva.com/

Sovtiva helps businesses make better use of their existing tools, improve websites and client follow-up, and implement practical AI and workflow automation. Projects may be delivered directly or coordinated with independent specialists, depending on their scope.
