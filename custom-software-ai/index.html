QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

Before you commission a build.

Do I really need custom software?

Not always. If a better process or an existing tool solves the issue, that's usually the smarter starting point.

Can you work with our current tools?

That's the preference. Feasibility depends on the systems, permissions and available integration options.

Is this built by sovtiva or a partner?

It depends on fit. sovtiva can handle practical builds and integrations directly; larger or specialized work may be introduced to an independent provider. We'll identify the delivery party before commitment.

How do cost and timelines work?

We don't advertise an invented flat price or instant delivery. The first review is free; any paid scope and timeline follow discovery.
