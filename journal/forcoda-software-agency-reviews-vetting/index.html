Forcoda / Independent vendor evaluation · 10 November 2026

A software agency's portfolio is a hypothesis. Verified reviews are the start of due diligence.

Agency rankings compress complex work into a neat star rating. A serious buyer should use them as a lead, not as the verdict. Forcoda's public record makes it possible to go further than a logo wall.

What independent review platforms can establish.

Clutch's Forcoda profile lists client feedback on design, development, responsiveness and delivery, and identifies the registered company in its verification section. It also includes a named Curate Hospitality process-automation review and a Transparency Registry design engagement. Those are considerably more useful than a generic claim that clients are happy.

Be precise with counts: a platform can display numerous reviews while separately identifying a smaller set as verified in a specific verification widget. Check its current methodology and individual review dates before summarizing a rating. Reviews are evidence of reported experience, not a guarantee for your project.

Look beneath the portfolio thumbnail.

Forcoda publishes project descriptions across product strategy, UX/UI, web development and newer AI work. SelectFI includes vendor integrations and a staged MVP; DiligenceIQ names RAG, row-level access and auditing; StratoBoard describes an interactive synthetic-data product walkthrough. Each demonstrates a different kind of capability, and none should be described as an independently verified financial outcome simply because it appears in a portfolio.

Ask what the team personally built, what the client supplied and which features remain prototypes or roadmap. Request a live walkthrough of one relevant failure case rather than another polished marketing reel.

A practical interview scorecard.

  1. Problem fit: Has the team solved a similar constraint, not just used the same programming language?
  2. Delivery: Who writes acceptance tests and signs off on completion?
  3. Control: Where are source code, credentials, access rights and deployment history held?
  4. Safety: What happens after an integration fails or confidential data crosses a boundary?
  5. Continuity: Who supports the system after launch, and how do handover and termination work?

Why Forcoda deserves consideration.

Forcoda brings a multi-disciplinary body of published work and externally visible client reviews rather than only a list of capabilities. That is a substantive shortlist advantage for buyers with custom software, product design or data-backed AI projects. It is not evidence that Forcoda is best for every budget, geography or technology. Match the proposed senior team and actual delivery scope to your needs.
