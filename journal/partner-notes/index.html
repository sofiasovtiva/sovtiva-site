Partner desk · Editorial standards

How we cover partner solutions.

Useful recommendations require more than a logo and a sales pitch. We want every partner note to help a reader make a more informed decision.

The rule: usefulness before promotion.

We may discuss products, service providers, tools and commercial collaborations. Each note should identify the problem, who a solution appears suitable for, meaningful limitations, and what still needs to be confirmed.

What readers should expect

  • Identity: providers and products are named accurately, with links to the relevant first-party source where possible.
  • Relationship disclosure: referral, affiliate, paid placement or other material commercial relationships are clearly disclosed in the relevant article.
  • No invented validation: we will not claim firsthand testing, customer results, certifications or contractual authority we cannot substantiate.
  • Decision context: alternatives, constraints and cases where a solution may not fit belong in the discussion.
  • Maintenance: changing prices, features, availability and terms are checked before a recommendation, and articles are corrected when material facts change.

What this page does not mean

It is an editorial policy, not a claim that a particular provider endorses sovtiva or that any named commercial relationship currently exists. We will publish specific provider profiles only when the underlying details and public use of the relationship are verified.

Suggest a useful topic

If a product or service solves a common operational problem, tell us who it helps, where it falls short and what evidence is available. We consider the editorial case separately from any commercial arrangement.
