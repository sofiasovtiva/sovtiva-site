About sovtiva · November 3, 2026

What does sovtiva actually do?

We help businesses make everyday work easier. Sometimes that means improving a process. Sometimes it means a ready-to-use tool or an introduction to an independent specialist.

sovtiva is an independent business-solutions and creative venture brand based in Mexico City, working with people remotely. Our business work centers on practical workflow improvements, organized information and useful digital systems. Alongside that, we develop original products and creative projects.

1. Business workflows and information

Scattered spreadsheets, repetitive updates and unclear handoffs can make simple work harder than it needs to be. We help identify what is slowing a process down and consider a suitable next step: clearer data, a better operating routine, reporting or automation where it makes sense.

We also work on practical research and revenue-operations systems. The starting point is the work itself, not a particular software product.

2. Original digital tools

Some problems do not need a custom project. sovtiva creates downloadable templates and practical systems for tracking and organizing everyday business details. The catalog is available through our independent digital shop.

Explore the sovtiva shop ↗

3. Introductions to independent specialists

When a company needs custom software, a more involved AI workflow or other specialist technology support, the right provider may be outside our direct work. sovtiva maintains commercial referral and affiliate relationships with independent providers and can help identify a relevant conversation.

Important distinction: these providers are separate businesses. They are responsible for their own scoping, proposals, pricing and delivery. An introduction is not a guarantee of availability or results. Commercial relationships are disclosed where relevant.

4. Original ventures

We are building a wider portfolio, including Fieldnote Press publishing, original Spanish-first media, software concepts and an experimental one-of-one fashion atelier. Some of these projects are still in development. We distinguish concepts from products that are already available.
