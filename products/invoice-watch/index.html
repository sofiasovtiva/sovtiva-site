04 / TRANSPARENCY

What Invoice Watch does not claim

— Invoice alerts are signals for human review, not proof of overbilling or money recovered.

— The tool does not verify SAT invoice stamps, tax validity, or supplier legitimacy.

— PDF and Excel .xlsx uploads are not supported; use CSV or CFDI 4.0 XML.

— Invoice file contents are processed in the browser. Optional follow-up statuses can be saved locally in that browser.

— Public customer sign-up and live billing are not active yet.
