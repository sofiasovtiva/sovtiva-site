## Findings

HyperFrames: HTML/CSS to MP4 with CLI, browser & FFmpeg; Apache-2.0, Node>=22; separate paid model services. https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes
Motion Canvas: TypeScript animation & editor; MIT; suited to vector explainers and narrated diagrams. https://github.com/motion-canvas/motion-canvas
