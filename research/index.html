Our research standard

Curiosity is cheap.
Proof takes work.

Every investigation should improve something outside the article itself.

01 / QUESTION

Ask something difficult.

Start with a buyer problem, technical uncertainty or real constraint.

02 / EVIDENCE

Check the source.

Separate documented facts, our inference and what remains unknown.

03 / TEST

Build when possible.

Reproduce a bounded workflow with permitted tools and record failures too.

04 / APPLY

Change the operation.

Turn valid findings into better delivery, offers, tools or decisions.
