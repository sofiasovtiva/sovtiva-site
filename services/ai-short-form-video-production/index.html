Frequently asked questions

Is it fully automated?

No. We use software to reduce repetitive work, with human checks for editorial choices, subtitle accuracy, rights and approval.

Can you edit YouTube videos?

Only if you own the necessary rights or have permission. A public video link is not a commercial reuse license.

Will you publish on our accounts?

We can scope scheduling and distribution separately. Publishing is never implied by an editing brief and requires explicit authorization.

How much does it cost?

We quote against the footage, number of deliverables, complexity, revisions and distribution requirements. No unvalidated package price or guaranteed return.

Will we earn money for the views?

Some funded creator campaigns exist, but they are separate from production services. Eligibility, caps, budgets and approval mean earnings are never guaranteed.
