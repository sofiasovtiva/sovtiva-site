Built around a field-service workflow, not a general chatbot.

WHERE TO LOOK FIRST

Three practical workflows to test

01

Service request to work order

Collect equipment, address and urgency accurately, check eligibility and create a work order with a traceable source.

02

Dispatch and exceptions

Match jobs to technicians by location, skill and availability while sending conflicts or urgent situations to a human dispatcher.

03

Warranty and billing

Preserve required documentation, flag uncertain coverage and prepare invoices or claims for review before submission.

FROM SCOPING TO PROOF

What a serious HVAC pilot should prove

These are Sovtiva's evaluation criteria, not audited customer results or guarantees. Capabilities depend on the actual tool stack and agreement.

    Choose one job class

    Select a specific service request or warranty process. Record baseline completion time, failed handoffs and source systems.

  2. 02
    Validate actual integration

    Check field-service software, service areas, job permissions, customer notifications and the handling of sensitive customer information.

  3. 03
    Test real exceptions

    Reproduce missed appointments, invalid addresses, ineligible warranties and repeat webhook calls. Count correct completions and manual recoveries.
