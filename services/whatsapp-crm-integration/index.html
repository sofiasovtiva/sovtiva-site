WhatsApp Business and CRM integration
A message should become a managed request.

If new customer questions arrive in WhatsApp but disappear between reception, sales and support, your problem is often the handoff. A useful integration records the right information, assigns an owner and keeps the customer in control.

THE STARTING POINT

Start with the exact conversation path.

A WhatsApp-to-CRM project may involve a simple link and manual intake, or the official Business Platform, a supported provider and an event-based integration. We map how a contact enters, what consent exists, where a record is stored and who handles exceptions before choosing technology.

Business-initiated messages on the WhatsApp Business Platform generally need approved templates; other customer service replies follow the platform's service-window rules. Those boundaries matter for appointment reminders, marketing messages and support. An automation that ignores them can harm the customer experience and put account access at risk.

WHERE TO LOOK FIRST

Three workflows with a clear purpose

01

Capture without duplicates

Create or update the correct CRM record for an authorized inquiry, with a source and a responsible team member.

02

Coordinate appointments

Connect scheduling and confirmations to the customer record, including cancellations, human review and approved communication paths.

03

Hand over difficult cases

Keep complaints, safety issues or unusual situations with people, while moving the context into a queue they can act on.

Can we automate follow-ups?

Only where consent, policy, templates and appropriate review allow it. Some customer communications should remain human-led.
