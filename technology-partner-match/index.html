OPEN ABOUT THE MODEL

Good referrals
need trust.

Is SOVTIVA the company doing the build?

Not necessarily. For independent introductions, the provider is responsible for its own scope, price and delivery. We'll say who is responsible before you proceed.

Does SOVTIVA receive referral compensation?

Some relationships may generate affiliate or referral compensation. We disclose relevant commercial context rather than hiding the relationship.

Will you send my information to partners automatically?

No. An initial inquiry is not a registered referral. We seek your agreement before sharing contact details, and verify applicable registration requirements.

What if the network is not right for my project?

We'll say so. The goal is a better decision, even when that means not making an introduction.
